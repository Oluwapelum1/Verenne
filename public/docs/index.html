Version 1.0.0

A slow, botanical storefront, set up in an afternoon.

Verenne is an editorial Shopify theme for small-batch beauty and wellness brands: an asymmetric product grid, a full-page product dossier with a magnifier and a 3D glass bottle, and a dawn-to-dusk ritual dial.

Getting started

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes, add Verenne and choose Customize.
  2. Under Theme settings, pick your Heading font and Body font and set the dark and light color schemes. The defaults are Bodoni Moda and Figtree on warm ink and travertine.
  3. On the home page, choose the Product collection section's collection and add a Filter block for each category you want as a tab.
  4. Add Step blocks to the Ritual dial, one per product, set to the first or second routine (for example Dawn and Dusk).
  5. Create menus named main-menu and footer under Content → Menus; the header and footer use them automatically.
  6. Optionally add the product details metafield to unlock the dossier's specs, texture swatch, scent notes and 3D bottle.

Product details

Everything in the theme works with standard product data: title, description, images, variants and price. The dossier and product page show extra rows when a product has a verenne.details metafield of type JSON. Each field is optional; rows without data are hidden.

  1. Go to Settings → Custom data → Products and add a definition: namespace and key verenne.details, type JSON.
  2. Open a product and paste its details into the new field:
{
  "subtitle": "Active botanical serum",
  "latin": "Helichrysum stoechas",
  "texture": "Weightless golden micro-oil",
  "notes": [
    "Immortelle",
    "Neroli",
    "Cedar"
  ],
  "keyIngredients": [
    "Corsican Helichrysum",
    "Rosehip seed"
  ],
  "benefits": [
    "Replenishes the lipid barrier",
    "Lit-from-within radiance"
  ],
  "howToUse": "Warm 3–4 drops between palms and press into damp skin.",
  "ph": "5.2 (Skin-neutral)",
  "finish": "Lit-from-within satin",
  "glow": "#F2A65A",
  "glass": {
    "shape": "dropper",
    "color": "#7A3F12",
    "liquid": "#E08A2E"
  },
  "moment": "Dawn",
  "synergy": {
    "pairId": "brume-botanique",
    "benefit": "Press into damp skin so the oil spreads more easily"
  },
  "description": "A one or two sentence summary shown in the product dossier."
}
FieldShown as
subtitleItalic line under the product name, and on product cards
ph, finishThe two specification boxes
texture, glowTexture row with an animated swatch in the glow color
notes, keyIngredients, benefits, howToUseScent, key botanicals, numbered benefits and how-to rows
glassThe 3D bottle: shape is dropper, jar, mist, pump or tube; color is the glass, liquid the contents
momentGroups products for “Pairs well with” in the home page dossier
synergy“Recommended partner” with a button that adds both products. pairId is the partner's handle
descriptionShort summary used in the dossier instead of the full description

Product titles that start with a number such as N°01 Éclat Sauvage show the number separately and the name in large type.

Home page content

The home page is built from sections you can reorder, hide or duplicate: Hero, Product collection, Manifesto, Ritual dial, Origins, Press and FAQ. Lists such as manifesto standards, origins, quotes and questions are blocks, so you can add or remove entries freely. The hero button can show your lowest price: write [price] in its label.

Clicking a product on the home page opens the product dossier in place. Product cards everywhere else link to the full product page.

Menus and anchors

Links to sections on the home page scroll smoothly. Use the section's anchor, for example /#collection, /#ritual, /#origins or /#faq. Until you create a main-menu, the header shows these four links. The full-screen menu also lists your products and includes predictive search.

Filters and search

Collection and search pages use Shopify's storefront filtering. Choose which filters appear (availability, price, product type, tags, variant options or metafields) in the free Search & Discovery app. The same app sets complementary products for the “Complete the ritual” recommendations on product pages.

Reviews

Star ratings appear on product pages and in the dossier when products have Shopify's standard review metafields (reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count). Most review apps write these automatically. Without review data, no stars are shown.

Countries and languages

The footer's language and currency control switches the storefront when you sell in several markets or languages. Add them under Settings → Markets and Settings → Languages; the control updates on its own.

Editing theme text

Headings and section text are edited in the theme editor. Shopper-facing labels such as “Add to bag”, “Bag”, “Checkout”, the empty-bag message and delivery notes live in the theme's language file: go to Online Store → Themes → ⋯ → Edit default theme content and search for the text. Translations for other languages are added the same way, or with the Translate & Adapt app.

Accessibility

The theme scores 100 for accessibility in Lighthouse on the home, product and collection pages. Menus, the bag and the dossier close with Escape, every control is reachable by keyboard with a visible focus outline, motion is reduced for visitors who ask for it, and the default colors meet WCAG AA contrast. If you change colors, keep body text at a contrast of at least 4.5:1.
