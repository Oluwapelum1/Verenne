## 1. Product Photography & Image Provenance

All product visuals included in `/public/products/` were created specifically and exclusively for the VERENNE storefront template. **No stock photography, scraped web imagery, trademarked logos, or unauthorized third-party digital assets were used.**

### Generation & Rendering Pipeline
- **Engine**: Generative AI Studio Rendering via Google Imagen 3 (High-Resolution Photographic Model).
- **Art Direction & Lighting Specification**:
  - Photographed at 5500K balanced daylight with secondary 2800K warm champagne fill and upper-left key light.
  - Specular rim highlights on authentic 380–420nm Swiss biophotonic Miron amber and deep indigo cosmetic glass.
  - Custom textures: Micro-droplets on frosted cosmetic jars, brushed brass closures, and matte obsidian dropper bulbs.
  - Zero real-world logos or trademarked insignia: Every render features the clean, custom-embossed brand mark "VERENNE".
- **Post-Processing**: Backgrounds were extracted using clean alpha-channel edge feathering and paired with layered contact shadows. Responsive WebP derivatives (320px thumbnail, 600px shelf card, and full-resolution uncompressed master) were compiled with Python Pillow.
